At a glance

Situation
Genesys needed to keep its Leader position in the two most important contact centre analyst reports, with strong evidence from every region.

Contributed the JAPAC perspective: briefing prep, submission content, new use cases, and customer references.

Context

Genesys sells contact centre and customer experience software to enterprises worldwide. I led PMM and marketing for India and Asia, and supported global analyst conversations as part of the global PMM team.

In the contact centre market, the Gartner Magic Quadrant and the Forrester Wave strongly shape enterprise shortlists. Genesys had been a Leader in the Magic Quadrant for many years, so the goal was to keep that position while the market shifted towards AI.

The problem

Holding a Leader position is its own challenge. Analysts expect a leader to show where it is heading next, with evidence from customers in every major region, not just North America and Europe.

Genesys also had to explain how it approached AI at a time when competitors were racing to release AI features quickly.

My role

I owned

The JAPAC input for analyst evaluations: briefing preparation, submission content, new use cases from the region, and customer references

I worked with

Internally: customer success, the product team, and the Customer Advisory Board

Externally: customers, the analyst relations team, and the analysts themselves

Approach: key decisions

  1. Position Genesys as an experience orchestrator

    Genesys positioned itself as orchestrating the whole customer experience, covering every touchpoint rather than just the contact centre. I contributed learnings from APAC and the results of positioning beta tests run in the region.

    Why: Analysts evaluate vision as well as features. A clear, tested position on where customer experience is heading gives them a reason to rate a vendor as a leader, and regional test results showed the positioning worked with real buyers.

  2. Make ethical AI and safety a strength

    We highlighted Genesys's approach to ethical and responsible AI: new AI capabilities can reach the market a little later than some competitors', because they are tested to be the safest.

    Why: Without context, a slower release pace can look like falling behind. Framing it around safety and testing showed analysts it was a deliberate choice that protects enterprise customers.

  3. Bring JAPAC evidence into global submissions

    For both the Gartner and Forrester evaluations, I prepared briefing material, submission content, new use cases, and customer references from JAPAC.

    Why: Analysts want proof of success across regions. Evidence from JAPAC showed Genesys delivering for customers well beyond its core markets.

What I built

Results

  • Genesys named a Leader in the 2025 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Contact Center as a Service, its 11th consecutive year as a Leader
  • Genesys named a Leader in The Forrester Wave: Contact Center as a Service Platforms, Q2 2025
  • JAPAC use cases and customer references included in the evidence Genesys gave analysts
