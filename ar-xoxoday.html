Context

Xoxoday offers rewards, incentives, and employee engagement software, with three products, including Empuls and Plum. I handled global product marketing for Empuls, along with brand and field marketing, including analyst relations.

The problem

Two things were happening at once. The company was raising funds, and it was entering new markets and customer segments: mid-market companies and large enterprises.

Both investors and larger buyers look for outside validation. Xoxoday had three products, two of them game-changers in HR tech and sales revenue, yet it had no analyst mentions. As a smaller company, it had to earn analysts' attention.

My role

I owned

Building the analyst relations function from the ground up: outreach, information for analysts, and ongoing follow-up.

Approach: key decisions

  1. Reach out proactively to many analysts

    Rather than waiting to be noticed, we contacted multiple analysts who covered our space.

    Why: A smaller company can't count on analysts finding it. Contacting several at once also improved the odds that at least one would include us in upcoming research.

  2. Give analysts everything they need

    We made it easy for analysts to understand Xoxoday by sharing complete, well-organized information about the platform, customers, and direction.

    Why: Analysts cover many vendors and have little time. Complete information upfront removed friction and made us easier to evaluate.

  3. Act on what analysts told us

    Analyst feedback showed that the overall platform positioning wasn't clear, and the differentiating strengths of the individual products weren't coming through. I revamped the platform storytelling and created more platform-specific demos showing both the platform and each product.

    Why: Analysts evaluate a clear story. Fixing the positioning they flagged made Xoxoday easier to place in their research.

  4. Follow up consistently

    We followed up with each analyst after outreach and briefings.

    Why: Analyst research runs on long cycles. Staying in touch kept Xoxoday in mind when reports were being planned.

What I built

Results

  • Mentioned in a Forrester Wave and by IDC within 6 months of starting the effort [exact report names]
  • Analyst validation supported the fundraise
  • Helped sales break into the mid-market and enterprise segments

What I'd do differently

I would have started a monthly analyst digest, sharing updates proactively between conversations. I later did this at Uniphore, and it made a real difference.
