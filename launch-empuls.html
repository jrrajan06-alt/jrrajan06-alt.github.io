Context

Empuls is Xoxoday's employee engagement product. Using a proprietary model, the business upgraded its existing eNPS survey into a Pulse Survey that listens to employee feedback across 51 factors.

The problem

The feature had been free. It now had to become a paid add-on for existing clients and attract new, feature-first customers, across both self-serve (PLG) and sales-led (SLG) motions.

My role

I owned

Positioning, and the full launch and GTM for the Pulse Survey across PLG and SLG.

I worked with

Users, CSMs, and prospects, whose feedback shaped the launch. [Anyone else?]

Approach: key decisions

  1. Learn why users weren't getting full value

    During the beta, I read support tickets and spoke to users, CSMs, and prospects to find out what stopped them using the feature fully.

    Why: You can't charge for a feature customers don't fully use. Finding the friction came first.

  2. Help customers act on the insights

    I mapped the friction points and created assets to help customers realize ROI, including three sets of action plans organizations could use once they had their survey results. Workflow changes went into the product, and the assets were placed along the customer journey.

    Why: Survey results only have value if organizations act on them. The action plans turned insights into outcomes.

  3. Run one launch for two motions

    I used my four-pillar launch framework, and trained sales to walk sales-led prospects through the approach.

    Why: Self-serve users needed guidance in the product; enterprise buyers needed it from sales.

What I built

Results

  • Existing customers (PLG): 40% adopted the free trial, with 25% converting to paid
  • New customers (PLG): 20% adopted the free trial, with 15% converting to paid
  • 4 feature-first customers within one month of launch, and 10 within 3 months
  • Feature ARR grew from $0 to $1M, and adoption rose from 5% to 65% of paid customers
