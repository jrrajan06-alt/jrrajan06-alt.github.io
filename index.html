About me

Trained in mathematics, with a Master's in Applied Mathematics, I'm an AI-native product marketer with 9+ years of experience. For the past 4+ years I've worked at AI and automation companies, where "drinking our own champagne" was the norm, so I build AI into my own work, from research to competitive intelligence.

I've worked across product-led, sales-led, and mixed GTM motions for enterprises, SMBs, and solo and family businesses, and been part of several scale-up journeys. Today I work as a fractional PMM for startups in procurement tech, climate tech, hospitality, cybersecurity, and ed-tech.
