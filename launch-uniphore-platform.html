Context

Uniphore sells conversational AI to large enterprises for their contact centres. The launch introduced its unified CCaaS AI layer: a single AI stack covering everything a contact centre needs.

The problem

The arrival of LLMs had disrupted the contact centre market. Agent guidance, one of Uniphore's core areas, was under pressure because LLMs could now summarize conversations.

The market was also full of mergers and acquisitions, as every player tried to assemble a complete experience orchestration story. Standing out required a clear, credible position.

My role

I owned

Launch positioning, launch assets, and analyst conversations for the event.

I worked with

The VP of Product Marketing, and the GTM teams: solutions engineering, sales, marketing, BDRs, and customer success, along with the product team.

Approach: key decisions

  1. Agree on positioning before building anything

    Being a first mover, we first had to articulate what AI-powered experience orchestration would actually look like, and get agreement on it.

    Why: In a crowded market, launching assets before the story is settled leads to mixed messages. Defining the category first let Uniphore set the terms.

  2. Show the future, not just describe it

    We built demos that showed what we were talking about, and an AI Blueprint document to help organizations plan their transformation phase by phase.

    Why: Buyers struggled to picture their AI journey. A phased plan made the vision practical and gave them a next step.

  3. Bring analysts into the launch

    Analyst conversations were set up in advance, so analysts were present during the event.

    Why: Independent voices at the launch added credibility that a vendor cannot create on its own.

Results

  • Positioned Uniphore early on AI-powered experience orchestration
