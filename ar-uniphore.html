Context

Uniphore sells conversational AI to large enterprises, mainly for contact centres and customer experience. I was the global PMM owner for U-Assist, the real-time agent assist product, and the platform PMM for JAPAC and EMEA.

For enterprise buyers, analyst research is part of due diligence. Before committing to a vendor, their evaluation teams check where it sits in reports from firms like Gartner and Forrester.

The problem

Uniphore had no active analyst relations function, and it wasn't being featured in many reports. That showed up in sales: enterprise customers needed analyst validation, and without it, deals took longer to close.

AR wasn't the only thing slowing the sales cycle, but it was a friction point we could remove.

Approach: key decisions

  1. Focus on the analysts our buyers actually read

    Rather than trying to brief every firm, I identified which analysts covered our market and influenced enterprise CX buyers, and prioritized those relationships.

    Why: Analyst time is limited, and so was ours. Mentions only help sales if they appear in the research buyers check during evaluations.

  2. Turn ad hoc contact into a repeatable process

    I streamlined how Uniphore engaged analysts, with a clear owner and a regular rhythm of briefings and updates.
