Context

Uniphore sells conversational AI to enterprise contact centres. With LLMs in the market, the contact centre industry was changing quickly.

The problem

Product marketing didn't yet have a clear view of the direction the market was taking, and leadership wanted strategic input for both product and company evolution.

My role

I owned

Creating a market assessment covering internal and external perspectives on the contact centre industry, to give strategic direction for growth.

I worked with

GTM teams (sales, CSMs, SEs, BDRs, and marketing), customers, and market analysts, as sources for the research. The PMM team ran the exercise together.

Approach: key decisions

  1. Hear from everyone close to the market

    The PMM team ran a four-week research exercise, getting on-the-ground perspectives from GTM teams, customers, market analysts, and a study of the competition.

    Why: Each group sees a different part of the market. Together they gave a complete picture.

  2. Focus the output on direction

    We created a strategy document showing what was working for us and in the industry, and the direction for growth.

    Why: Leadership needed decisions, not just data.

  3. Make it a regular exercise

    The assessment became a bi-annual initiative, which also informed the move to consumption pricing.

    Why: Markets shifting this fast needed regular check-ins.

What I built

Results

  • Two mergers and acquisitions in the language model space
  • Redirection and course correction on a couple of products with tech debt
  • Research that informed the move to consumption-based pricing
