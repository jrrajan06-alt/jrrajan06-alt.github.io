Context

As Uniphore scaled, every region had a growing number of partners and resellers selling its products.

The problem

Partners needed to get up to speed quickly and keep learning, with a single place to learn and find assets.

My role

I owned

Designing the partner onboarding program, ongoing enablement, and the partner repository

I worked with

RevOps, other PMMs, and solutions engineers

Approach: key decisions

  1. Tier partners by the value they drive

    With RevOps, I divided partners into three tiers based on the value they drive.

    Why: Not every partner needs the same investment. Tiers focused effort where it paid off.

  2. One onboarding program and one repository

    I created a comprehensive onboarding program on MindTickle, working with other PMMs and SEs, and hosted all assets there. Micro-lessons and certifications were enabled by tier.

    Why: Partners juggle many vendors. One place to learn and find everything made Uniphore easy to sell.

  3. A knowledge bot for partner questions

    I created a knowledge bot, trained on the product program, to act as a Q&A partner.

    Why: Partners got answers instantly, without waiting for a Uniphore contact.

What I built

Results

  • Partner ramp-up time reduced by 45%
  • Asset usage improved by 35%, with the knowledge bot surfacing relevant assets as needed
