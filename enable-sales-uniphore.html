Context

Uniphore's sales and BDR teams had expanded to around 400 people across regions.

The problem

A team that size needed a holistic sales enablement program: from onboarding to continuous support, with one place to learn and find assets.

My role

I owned

Designing the onboarding program, ongoing enablement and training, and the single enablement repository.

I worked with

RevOps, other PMMs, and solutions engineers.

Approach: key decisions

  1. One platform for learning and assets

    With RevOps, I built a comprehensive onboarding program on MindTickle, working with other PMMs and SEs, and hosted all assets there.

    Why: One place to learn and find assets meant reps stopped hunting through folders.

  2. Keep learning short and continuous

    I created micro-lessons on MindTickle for AEs, plus certifications to ensure minimum knowledge standards.

    Why: Busy sellers finish short lessons. Certifications made sure everyone met the same bar.

  3. Support reps during live calls

    I created a knowledge bot, trained on the product program, to act as a sales co-pilot during calls.

    Why: Reps can't remember everything. On-demand answers during calls kept deals moving.

What I built

Results

  • Sales ramp-up time reduced by 75%
  • 100% adoption of micro-lessons and course completion
  • Asset usage improved by 50%, with the knowledge bot surfacing the right assets when needed
