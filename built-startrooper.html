What it does

StarTrooper is an astro-gazing tourism guide. Based on your city, the time of year, and the time of night, it tells you which cosmic objects you can look for.

Why voice

Stargazing happens outdoors, in the dark, often with your hands full. Asking out loud and listening to the answer works better than reading a screen.

How it works

It follows a simple three-part voice design: ears, brain, and mouth.

PartWhat it doesBuilt with
EarsTurns your voice into textOpenAI Whisper
BrainWorks out what you can see, and writes the answerGPT-4o-mini
MouthSpeaks the answer back to youOpenAI TTS
