Context

Uniphore was entering two new regions, ANZ and MENA, with its Agent Assist product, working through contact centre platform (CCaaS) partners.

The problem

With generative AI, one half of the product, the summary feature, was now being offered by the CCaaS platforms themselves: the same partners Uniphore relied on to enter these markets.

If Uniphore competed with its partners, partner sellers would have little reason to sell it.

My role

I owned

Market-entry positioning, partner and channel selection, and the integrated campaigns for both regions.

I worked with

[Who did you work with? E.g. teams inside the company and anyone outside it]

Approach: key decisions

  1. Complement partners instead of competing with them

    I repositioned Uniphore to enter with a platform and analytics story.

    Why: Summaries were becoming a standard partner feature. Platform and analytics gave partners something to add to their offering, not a rival to it.

  2. Choose the right partners and channels

    I identified which partners and channels could best carry the story in each region.

    Why: In a new market, the right partner gives immediate access and credibility.

  3. Amplify entry with integrated campaigns

    Campaigns combined digital channels and events.

    Why: A mix of digital and in-person reached enterprise buyers who didn't know Uniphore yet.
